# Proposal

## Why

The current inbox feels austere and editorial rather than like a distinctive, welcoming workspace. Operators also need a comfortable appearance in different lighting conditions.

## What Changes

- Redesign the inbox, ticket detail, and feedback states as a warm creative workspace with moderate information density.
- Add a visible appearance control with system, light, and dark choices, remembered locally.
- Maintain readable, responsive, accessible interactions and the existing read-only ticket workflow.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `appearance-preferences`: Appearance selection, system preference following, and local persistence.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The SPA shell, styles, and ticket presentation are affected. The API, ticket data, and read-only behavior remain unchanged. Design work should use Impeccable guidance without introducing dependencies without a clear need.
