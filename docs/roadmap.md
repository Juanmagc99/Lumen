# Roadmap

Work through small OpenSpec changes. Do not create, modify or run tests unless explicitly requested by the user.

1. **Foundation:** structure, tools, configuration and a minimal API/frontend connection.
2. **Simulated journey:** inbox, detail, editing and approval with fictional data.
3. **Profiles and documents:** two example profiles, document management, persisted jobs and profile-scoped fragments.
4. **AI and RAG:** retrieval, LangGraph, separately configurable decision and response models, citations and insufficient-evidence handling.
5. **Observability and evaluation:** traces and, when requested, reproducible evaluation and measurement for both profiles.
6. **PDF and portfolio:** extractable-text PDF support, accessibility, setup documentation and demo material.

Kafka, Kubernetes and extra services are optional extensions. The foundation proposal reflects the English-language and user-directed testing requirements.
